Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • SRR Bazaar (GAJAWADA SUJATHA)

1. Order Cancellation

You can cancel your order free of charge before the product is dispatched from our workshop. To cancel an order, please message our support line on WhatsApp at +91 81850 12453 or call +91 99486 33303 with your Order ID. Once an order has been dispatched and assigned a courier tracking number, cancellation is not possible.

2. 7-Day Return & Replacement Policy

We take immense pride in the quality and packaging of our handcrafted jewellery, sarees, and decor items. However, if you receive a product that is defective, damaged during transit, or incorrect:

  • You are eligible for an immediate replacement or full refund within 7 days of delivery.
  • To report a damaged or defective item, please provide a clear 360-degree unboxing video recorded from the moment the sealed parcel is opened, showing the shipping label and the condition of the item.
  • Send the video and order details to our official WhatsApp support: +91 81850 12453 or email contact@srrbazaar.com.

3. Product Exchanges

Yes, we accept exchanges for damaged or defective items. If an identical replacement is unavailable in stock, you may choose an alternative item of equal value or request a 100% full refund.

4. Refund Method & Timeline

Once your return/replacement claim is verified and approved:

  • Refunds are initiated within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • The refund amount will be credited directly to your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Card) within 5 to 7 business days as per RBI guidelines.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are credited directly to your verified UPI ID or Bank account after you provide account details to our customer care team.
